Follow the law as it is made, and the landmark laws behind it, in the EU, the UK and the US: regulations and directives, delegated and implementing acts, bills and Acts of Parliament, federal and state statutes, executive orders and agency rules. For each file, see where it stands, what changes and which dates matter, explained for law students.

LexWatcher follows new and upcoming law in the European Union, the United Kingdom and the United States: EU regulations and directives, delegated and implementing acts, UK bills and Acts of Parliament, US federal and state statutes, executive orders and agency rules. Files include landmark laws such as the GDPR, the AI Act, the DSA, the DMA, the UK Online Safety Act and Section 230, and new files such as the Digital Omnibus, the GDPR reform, the Digital Fairness Act, UK bills from the 2026 King's Speech, and US legislation on crypto and artificial intelligence.

LexWatcher is a study tool, not legal advice. Every file links to its official or primary source: check it before relying on a date or a stage, because legislation moves fast. All information comes from publicly available sources: official legislative databases, official gazettes and publicly published analysis.
